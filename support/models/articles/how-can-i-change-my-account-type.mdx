This page shows how to change your W&B account plan. Plans include Free, Academic, Pro, Team, and Enterprise.

| Change | Self-service? | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free to Academic | Yes | Add your academic email, set it as your primary email, then submit an [academic application](https://wandb.ai/academic_application). You can also open this application from **Billing** on your account page. |
| Academic to Pro, Team, or Enterprise | No | Email [W&B Support](mailto:support@wandb.com) with your account email, organization name, and the plan you want to move to. |
| Pro, Team, or Enterprise to Academic | No | Email [W&B Support](mailto:support@wandb.com) with your account email, organization name, and the plan you want to move to. |

To change the email address on your account before changing plans, add the new email and set it as your primary email in your account settings.

See [W&B pricing](https://wandb.ai/site/pricing) for details on [academic license limitations](https://wandb.ai/site/pricing/#:~:text=What%20does%20the%20academic%20license%20include%3F).

## If the email you need is already on another account

You can attach an email address to only one W&B account. If the email you need for a plan change already belongs to another account, adding it either fails or creates a second account instead of linking to the one you want to keep. Email [W&B Support](mailto:support@wandb.com) to have the other account deleted and its email released, then continue with the change above.

<Warning>
Deleting an account also deletes any projects and runs logged to it. Move anything you want to keep before you ask W&B Support to delete the account.
</Warning>

## If your Academic plan seems to be missing

If your organization switched to a trial plan and your Academic plan appears to be gone, the academic organization typically still exists alongside the new one. Switch back to it from the organization dropdown in the upper right of the W&B UI before contacting W&B Support.
