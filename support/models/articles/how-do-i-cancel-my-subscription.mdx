---
title: "How do I cancel my subscription?"
keywords: ["Administrator", "billing", "subscription", "cancel"]
---

Subscription cancellation isn't self-service in the W&B app. To cancel, contact the W&B support team with enough information to identify your account.

To request cancellation:

1. From the email address associated with your account, email [support@wandb.com](mailto:support@wandb.com).
2. Include the following details so the team can locate your account:
   - Organization name
   - Email address associated with the account
   - Username

Support confirms your request and follows up with next steps. For related actions such as viewing your current plan, invoices, or the billing admin on file, see [Manage billing settings](/platform/app/settings-page/billing-settings).
