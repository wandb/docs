title: "How can I change between academic and Pro, Team, and Enterprise account types?"
keywords: ["Administrator", "Academic", "User Management", "Billing"]
---

You can change your W&B account among the Free, academic, Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans. The steps depend on which plans you are changing between.

## Change from a Free plan to an academic plan

Only a Free Plan can be upgraded to an Academic plan by applying:

1. In your account settings, add your academic email and set it as your primary email.
2. On the [W&B academic application page](https://wandb.ai/academic_application), submit your application for review. You can also open this application from **Billing** on your account page.

### If the academic email already belongs to another account

You can attach an email address to only one W&B account. If your academic email already has an account of its own, one of the following happens:

- Adding the academic email to your original account fails, because another account already uses that address.
- Linking the academic email leaves you with two separate accounts instead of one.

Both have the same cause, and the fix is to free the email address by deleting the account that holds it:

1. Sign in to the account that holds the academic email and delete any team it owns.
2. Ask W&B Support to delete that account, which releases the email address.
3. In the account you want to keep, open your account settings and add the academic email as an additional address.
4. Set the academic email as your primary email, then submit your academic application.

<Warning>
Moving the email doesn't move data. Deleting the account also deletes any projects and runs logged to it, so move anything you want to keep before you request the deletion.
</Warning>

### If your academic plan seems to be missing

If your organization changed to a trial plan and your academic plan appears to be gone, the academic organization typically still exists alongside the new one. Switch back to it from the organization dropdown in the upper right of the W&B UI before you contact W&B Support.

## Change from an academic plan to a Pro, Team, or Enterprise plan

Changing from an academic plan to a Pro, Team, or Enterprise plan isn't self-service. Contact W&B Support to make the change:

1. Open a support request from the [W&B contact page](https://wandb.ai/site/contact), or email W&B Support.
2. Include your account email, organization name, and the plan you want to move to.

W&B Support updates your account type and helps you complete any required billing setup.

To update the email address on your account before changing account types, link the new email and set it as your primary email in your account settings.

## Change from a Pro, Team, or Enterprise plan to an academic plan

Changing from a Pro, Team, or Enterprise plan to an academic plan isn't self-service. Contact W&B Support to make the change:

1. Open a support request from the [W&B contact page](https://wandb.ai/site/contact), or email W&B Support.
2. Include your account email, organization name, and the plan you want to move to.

See [W&B pricing](https://wandb.ai/site/pricing) for details on [academic license limitations](https://wandb.ai/site/pricing/#:~:text=What%20does%20the%20academic%20license%20include%3F).
